## What Jotacular is

A place to write things down quickly — typed, handwritten, spoken or
photographed — which can then be read by an AI agent you connect yourself. We
run the servers, store the notes, and make what you wrote legible. We do not
provide the agent, and the agent is not ours.

Keep your Google account secure, because anyone who can sign in as you can read

someone into a space you own, you are responsible for what they do in it.

**You own what you write.** Nothing in these terms transfers ownership of a note
to us.

To run the service we need your permission to do specific things with your notes:

access to, and send handwriting, voice and photographs to the recognition

That permission is limited to operating Jotacular for you. It ends when you delete
the content. It does not let us publish your notes, sell them, share them with
anyone else, or use them to train a model — ours or anybody's.

## Agents, and what you are taking on

This is the part most worth reading.

When you connect an agent you decide which spaces it can reach and what it may
do. **Anything it then does, it does with your authority.** An agent cannot reach
a space you cannot, and cannot hold a permission you did not give it — but within
that grant, its actions are yours.

You should also know that **note content can influence an agent that reads it.**
Text in a note — including a note somebody else put in a shared space, or text
inside a photograph — can be written specifically to redirect an AI agent into
doing something you did not intend. This is a real and unsolved problem across
the whole industry, not a Jotacular defect, and we cannot prevent it.

What we do about it: agent access defaults to reading and commenting rather than
editing, every agent action is recorded, nothing an agent does is permanent, and
you can revoke a grant instantly. What you should do about it: grant the narrowest
access that does the job, be careful about who can add notes to a shared space you
have connected an agent to, and check the record if something looks wrong.

## Paying

Free is a plan, not a trial, and it does not expire.

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance through Stripe. Prices are per space,
not per person — one price covers everyone you share it with, up to the number of
people that plan allows.

You can cancel at any time from your account. Cancelling stops the next charge
and you keep the paid features until the end of the period you have already paid
for. **We do not give partial refunds for time you have paid for and not used**,
except where the law where you live says otherwise — and if we have genuinely got
something wrong, write to us and we will sort it out.

If a payment fails we will retry and tell you. If it keeps failing the space
returns to the free plan. Your notes are not deleted, held hostage, or made
unreadable because a card expired.

## Limits

Storing notes is not metered. Write as many as you like, for as long as you like,
in as many spaces as you like.

What is metered is the work of reading something that is not already text: a page
of handwriting, a photograph, or a minute of audio. Each plan includes a monthly
allowance, and the current numbers are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

**Running out never costs you a note.** Capture keeps working, the recording and
the page you drew are kept exactly as they are, and they are read when the month
turns over. We will never refuse to save something you wrote because of a billing
limit.

## The person

Priya Raghunathan, 29, she/her. Produces two podcasts, one about county cricket and
one about her mother's generation of nurses who came over in the sixties. She
records on a Zoom H5, edits in Reaper, and does everything else in voice memos
because her hands are always holding something.

**Technical level: middling-high.** Comfortable with audio tools, impatient with
anything that makes her read.

**What she is nervous about.** Losing a line somebody said. Her whole job is that
the good sentence was captured, and once she has moved on she will not get it back.

**What made her look today.** She has 340 voice memos on her phone with names like
`New Recording 219` and she cannot find the one where her aunt talked about the
boat.

### The two findings, and why they matter more than the acts that did not run

**[031](issues/031-every-photo-and-every-voice-note-failed-in-the-browser.md) — every
photo and every voice note failed in the browser.** No CORS on the API at all, so the
preflight 404'd and the upload never left the device. **Two of the product's four
advertised capture modes — Snap and Speak — had never worked from the UI on this
machine**, under a fully green board, because no suite uses a browser. Fixed; her
whiteboard is on her canvas.

**[030](issues/030-a-capture-that-was-merely-waiting-was-reported-as-unreadable.md) —
a capture that was merely waiting was reported as unreadable.** A block deferred for
being over allowance fell through to `_[from a voice note, nothing legible on it]_` —
the one sentence docs/01 promises never to say, said to her and to her agent.
Seventy-six blocks in this database were already in that state. Fixed.

### The promise act 6 exists to test: kept

> Capture still works, recognition queues until next cycle, and we say so plainly.
> **We never refuse a capture.**

**Nothing anywhere refuses a capture.** There is no quota check on any write path.
The only thing an exhausted allowance changes is whether the reading happens now or
next month — the job is deferred to `app_period_start() + 1 month`, `attempts` is
**decremented** so a deferral is not a failure, and the artifact is untouched.

**An `anon` space proves it structurally**: `app_plan_allowance('anon')` is **0**, so
every anonymous draft is over quota from the moment it exists — and the marketing
hero writes to one of those every day. The most-used capture path in the product
runs permanently over quota and has never refused anything.

### What was NOT checked, and why

| | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Every transcript, of every recording** | `SPEECH_PROVIDER=fake`. There is no microphone and no transcriber |
| **Every caption, of every photo** — including the dark one and the small print | `VISION_PROVIDER=fake`. **This is act 4's actual point**: what does it say when it could not read something? Unanswerable here |
| **The screen at 99, 100 and 101 units** | Not reachable. The fake providers make every capture cost the minimum 1 unit, and one surface is capped at 8 (`MAX_TILES / TILES_PER_UNIT`), so crossing 100 would take a hundred captures by hand |
| **Whether she is warned before or only after** | `PlanSection` shows its warning only once `over` is true, so the answer *looks* like "after" — **reasoned from the code, not measured** |
| **The boat sentence, and the two years** | Both need real transcripts. Act 8's question — whether an agent says "she said 1962 and then 1964" or picks one and sounds confident — is the most useful thing this persona could have found out, and it is **not checked** |
| **8 photos** | One was uploaded, to prove the path. The seven that test the recognizer need a recognizer |

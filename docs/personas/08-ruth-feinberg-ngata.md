## The person

Ruth Feinberg-Ngata, 52, she/her. Runs a two-person garden design practice with her
business partner. Been using Jotacular since it launched, on a Solo plan, and pays
for it without thinking about it — which is the position every subscription wants
to be in and the position that ends the moment somebody looks at their bank
statement.

**Technical level: middling.** Competent, unsentimental, and entirely willing to
export everything and move to something else on a Sunday afternoon.

**What she is nervous about.** Being locked in. She has two years of plant lists,
client preferences and site notes in here and she wants to know she can take them
out before she commits to another year.

**What made her look today.** Her annual renewal is in eleven days and she is doing
the thing where she cancels four subscriptions.

## The situation

**Two years of notes** — the only persona with a history.

- **At least 60 notes**, spread across two years, not one afternoon
- Two plan changes behind her and two more coming
- **Inconvenient for the software:** she does not want to add anything. She wants
  to **read old things, change her plan, and find out whether she can get out.**
  Every other persona is building; she is auditing

### Act 3 — the note from the first week

### Act 4 — she checks she is not trapped

**Done when:** the export is opened and read, and the run has recorded what format

another Jotacular could read is not an export.

### Act 5 — she upgrades to see

Solo → Family. Stripe test mode.

**Done when:** on `family`, on screen and in the data, and the run has recorded

### Act 6 — she changes her mind

Family → Solo, the same day.

**Done when:** back on `solo`, and the run has recorded what she was refunded or
credited, what the screen said about it, and **whether the seat count and allowance
went back down**.

### Act 7 — the thing that goes wrong for her

She cancels outright. Eleven days before renewal, from `/account`.

**Done when:** cancelled, and the run has recorded: what the app said would happen,
what actually happened, when the space dropped to free, and **whether the two
sentences match**. If the app promised something about her data that is not true,
that is a `major` at minimum.

### Act 8 — the audit after the fall

Back on free. Open every part of the space.

**Done when:** all 60+ notes are confirmed present and searchable, her handwriting
and photos still render, and every capability she lost is recorded with the exact
words the app used to tell her she had lost it. **Any note that is gone, truncated,
or unreadable is a `blocker` and stops the run.**

### Act 9 — the stale session

Sign out. Come back the next day to a deep link into a note. Then let a session go
stale and see what `/signin?stale=1` says.

**Done when:** both recorded, including whether she ended up back where she was
going.

### Act 10 — the verdict

Write the paragraph.

**Done when:** it is written, honestly, and says whether she renews.

## Standing checks

**Wrong moves.** Three, named:

1. Click cancel, then immediately re-subscribe before the webhook lands.
2. Export the space twice at once.
3. Delete a note that has a comment and an arrow attached, then look for both.

**Reload and deep link.** F5 on `/account` in the gap between paying and the
webhook. Open a two-year-old note's URL after cancelling.

**Dates.** The 29 February note, and the `23:58` / `00:02` pair. Ask her agent what
she wrote "on the 29th" and check the arithmetic by hand. Then check whether the
renewal date the app shows matches what Stripe test mode thinks. Machine timezone,
stated.

**The count and the bill.** The hardest one in the roster, and it must be computed
by hand first: **three plan changes in one cycle.** What is she charged, what is
prorated, what does the allowance do on each change, and does the allowance reset
or carry? Write the expected numbers down, then open Account › Plan and usage.
Check the prices against docs/01: solo £5, family £9, annual is two months free.
**Where they disagree, the software is wrong.**

**The other side.** **Whoever she moves to.** The export file is the other side of
this transaction — open it in something that is not Jotacular and record whether
two years of work is really in there.

**Without a mouse.** One full job by keyboard: cancel the subscription, from
`/account` to confirmation, focus ring visible throughout.

**Somebody else's data.** Search for `gate code` and `DMG-01` — P07's words.
Deep-link a P07 note id. And because she has been through three plans, try it once
**while on free** and once **while on family**, in case an entitlement check is
doing tenancy's job. Expected: nothing, every time.
